Rome, Italy Robotics · 3D Vision

3D Vision & Robotics Researcher

I develop autonomous UAV perception systems for agricultural mapping, combining visual localization, SLAM, and dense 3D reconstruction. At the Italian Institute of Technology, I worked on 3D Gaussian Splatting for robotic teleoperation, autonomous artifact scanning, and LiDAR–camera fusion for 3D detection. I earned my Ph.D. at the University of Genoa and the Italian Institute of Technology.

Research Areas

3D Mapping and Reconstruction

Visual localization, SLAM, structure from motion, and neural scene representations for field-scale mapping and real-time visualization.

Robotic Perception and Autonomy

UAV sensing and mission control, 6-DoF object pose estimation, robot motion planning, and automated 3D scanning.

Multimodal 3D Vision

Learning-based fusion of camera and LiDAR data for 3D detection, and geometric methods for object localization across multiple views.
